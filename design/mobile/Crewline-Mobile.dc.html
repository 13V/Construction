4Safety is the thinnest ice here

Not backed — the sign-on itself. A safety record can only be a JHA, an incident, a toolbox talk or a hazard. There is no SWMS and no site induction, so neither document on ② has a row to live in and neither signature has anywhere to land. That is a schema change, not a screen.

Worker app · daily log and dockets · 390 × 844

The daily log and the dockets

Typing with gloves on does not happen, so the voice note is the primary input and the keyboard is the fallback. The app drafts the log from what it already knows — hours, photos, deliveries — and the worker corrects it. It never posts one on his behalf silently, because a log nobody read is a log nobody will defend in a dispute. Same posture on a docket: everything extracted is shown for review before it is saved, and the uncertain fields say so.

4Sources, and the weather problem
{{ r.k }} {{ r.col }} {{ r.note }}
Not backed — the weather. There is a free-text weather column on a log and a lat/lng on every site, but nothing joins them. "Auto-filled from the site's location" needs a weather service and a decision about which observation — the 7 am reading, the day's range, or rain since midnight. I have drawn the range plus rainfall, because that is what a delay claim argues about.
Half backed: a docket carries one confidence number for the whole read, not one per field. The four amber fields on ③ need per-field confidence, or the rule becomes cruder: below a threshold, nothing is pre-filled at all.

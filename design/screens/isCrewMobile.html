Crew app — my jobs, and getting photos off the phone

A framer needs four things: where am I today, what am I doing, what does the plan say, and here's a photo of what I found. Every photo carries its own GPS stamp, timestamp and uploader, so it lands in the right job folder without anyone in the office sorting it. Gloves on, one hand, in the sun — nothing on these screens is smaller than a thumb.

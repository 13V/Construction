company: **3 office users** (owner, office manager, PM/estimator) + **17 field workers**.

## The math, product by product

**JACK** — $299/mo base including one Full Access user, then $18 per additional Full Access and $8 per Mobile Only.
`$299 + (2 × $18) + (17 × $8) = $471/mo`
Annual billing gives one month free → `$471 × 11 = $5,181/yr`, an effective $432/mo.
If you put all 20 on Full Access instead it's $641/mo — so the seat mix matters enormously.
**Subs, vendors and clients are free seats.**

**Workyard** — $50/mo company base fee plus per-user.
Starter `$50 + (20 × $8) = $210/mo`; annual rate drops to $6/user → `$170/mo`.
Pro `$50 + (20 × $16) = $370/mo`.
⚠️ **Automatic worksite detection — the geofenced auto clock-in — is gated to the top "Autopilot" tier, which is custom-quoted.** So the published Workyard numbers are *not* comparable to our core feature. Their real price for it is unpublished and higher.

**busybusy** — $40/mo admin license which includes the first user, then per-user on top.
Pro `$40 + (19 × $9.99) = $230/mo`. Premium `$40 + (19 × $14.99) = $325/mo`.
Annual billing is discounted but the rate isn't published. Clock-in photo verification sits in Pro.

**ClockShark** — Standard `$40 + (20 × $9) = $220/mo`. Pro `$60 + (20 × $11) = $280/mo`.
GPS and geofencing are in both tiers. **A three-year contract term applies to all plans** — unusual for this market and worth raising with anyone comparing.

**Fieldwire** — flat per-user with no discounted field seat: $39 / $64 / $89 per user per month on annual billing. At 20 users that's $780–$1,780/mo. Monthly billing costs more again. Priced for GCs and project teams, not for crews.

**Buildertrend** — dropped its published Essential/Advanced/Complete tiers in 2026 for volume-based custom quotes. Third-party estimates put it at roughly $339–$1,099/mo. Critically it's **flat-rate with unlimited users**, so headcount doesn't move the price.

## What this says about pricing our product

**The pure GPS time-tracking band is $170–$370/mo at 20 users.** That's tight, and it's the ceiling if we're seen as "another time clock."

**Jack sits at $432–$471** — roughly double the trackers — because it's a business platform. That's the price of being seen as running the business, not watching the crew.

**The seat model is the real lever.** A construction company is ~85% field workers. Jack charges $8 for a mobile-only seat and nothing for subs and clients; Fieldwire charges $64 for everyone and is 4× the price as a result. Any product sold into crews needs a cheap field tier or the math kills it.

**Suggested position:** since this app does both categories, it can defend the space between them — something like a **$99–$149/mo base + $20–25 per office user + $8–12 per field user**, landing near **$300–$400/mo at 20 users**. That undercuts Jack while sitting above every tracker, which is honest: it does more than they do.

Two competitive angles worth using: **no long contract** (against ClockShark's three-year term), and **auto clock-in included at every tier** (against Workyard gating it to Autopilot).
